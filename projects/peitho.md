# Peitho

Peitho explores how to help clients who are unsure of their taste clarify their preferences and narrow down the aesthetic directions they are drawn to.

## The problem

A client may recognise that they like a work without being able to explain why, or may find it difficult to describe what they would like to see next. Peitho investigates how guided questions can make those preferences easier to articulate.

The current design uses everyday choices and written descriptions to explore preferences in colour, composition, materials, and subject matter, including what a client dislikes.

## Current work

The project has a written questionnaire draft, a framework for interpreting responses, and a validation plan. The proposed output is a preference profile that can support further discussion with the client.

Peitho is in the research and design stage. Software implementation and participant validation are planned. Artwork matching is outside the first version's scope.
